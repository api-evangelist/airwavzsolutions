# Airwavz Solutions

> Airwavz Solutions designs, builds, owns, and operates in-building wireless infrastructure for property owners across the United States.

Airwavz serves commercial office, healthcare, hospitality, multifamily, and event venue properties nationwide, with over 154 million square feet under agreement. Founded in 2013 and headquartered in Charlotte, NC, with regional offices in Atlanta, Houston, and Dallas.

## Key Facts
- **Venue-funded model:** Airwavz funds, designs, installs, owns, and operates the wireless system—typically with no upfront capital cost for building owners.
- **Neutral-host:** One shared infrastructure supports all major wireless carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) simultaneously.
- **Carrier Partnerships:** Master License Agreements (MLA) and Master Service Agreements (MSA) are in place with all three national carriers.
- **Operations:** Every system includes 24/7/365 proactive monitoring, remote diagnostics, and guaranteed performance standards.
- **Contact:** +1-833-AIRWAVZ (247-9289) | info@airwavz.com

## Solutions
- [Cellular (DAS)](https://airwavz.com): Neutral-host distributed antenna systems delivering carrier-grade indoor cellular coverage for all major carriers, engineered for 4G LTE and 5G.
- [Public Safety (ERRCS)](https://airwavz.com): Emergency Responder Radio Communication Systems designed to pass AHJ testing and inspections.
- [Wi-Fi](https://airwavz.com): Managed Wi-Fi networks for tenants, guests, and building operations.
- [Fiber](https://airwavz.com): Core fiber backbone providing converged infrastructure for cellular, Wi-Fi, and smart building applications.
- [Smart Networks](https://airwavz.com): Secure private wireless networks supporting IoT and smart building systems, designed for 5G and beyond.
- [Wireless Carriers](https://airwavz.com): Details on how Airwavz partners with wireless carriers, building owners, and developers.

CHARLOTTE, N.C. – April 2025 –Airwavz Solutions is proud to announce the promotion of David Saab to Executive Vice President (EVP), Chief Legal and Risk Officer (CLRO). This promotion recognizes David’s influential leadership at Airwavz as well as his extensive experience in the commercial real estate, healthcare, and wireless industries.
